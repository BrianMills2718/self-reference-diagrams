Reading self-reference diagrams: a step-by-step tutorial

Thirteen short lessons, from one sentence about the weather up to paradoxes, other logics and valid arguments. Every lesson uses the same few marks: a sentence box; its two suppositions, T (suppose it is true) and F (suppose it is false); and the possible worlds, where each supposition leads to a region. When sentences talk about other sentences, arrows show how one supposition leads to another.
